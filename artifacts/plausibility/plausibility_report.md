## Honesty audit: PAM sites vs occupancy-competent competitors

- **Total NGG PAM sites** in a human diploid genome are ~4×10^8 (one every ~8 bp, both strands). This is NOT the competitor count.
- **Occupancy-competent competitors** (M here) are the far smaller subset of loci that actually bind and hold Cas9 long enough to sequester it. Empirical off-target assays (GUIDE-seq / CIRCLE-seq) typically detect tens to low-hundreds of cleavage-active off-targets per guide; the number that transiently *sequester* Cas9 is unknown but is bounded by that order, not by the 10^8 PAM count.
- **We do NOT assume 10^4 effective competitors is realistic.** M=10^4 occupancy-competent loci for a single guide has no empirical support and is treated here as an upper-bound stress point only.
- **Controlling unknown:** the effect is governed by the ratio 2M/Nc = (occupancy-competent off-target copies) / (active nuclear Cas9-RNP molecules). Both numerator and denominator are unmeasured in most experiments. Relevance now hinges on ONE composite unknown: **whether occupancy-competent off-target loci can rival or exceed the active nuclear Cas9-RNP molecule count.** Absolute timescale (set by volume + Cas9 conc) is a secondary honesty flag: at scarce Cas9 the delays play out over 10^5-10^7 s, longer than RNP lifetime / cell cycle, so a large % delay may be biologically moot.

## Verdict rationale

Plausibility envelope: occupancy-competent loci M ≤ 1000, active Cas9 ≥ 100 molecules. Grid cells with a strong effect (>5 pp or >25% t50): 61; of those inside the plausibility envelope: 0; weak-effect cells inside envelope: 16.

## Adversarial interpretation

**The molecule-count ratio 2M/Nc governs the effect** (competitor target-strand
copies per active Cas9 molecule). Volume shifts absolute concentration (hence
S_site and timescale) but the phase boundary tracks the ratio; smaller nuclei
(250 fL) push effects up modestly via higher S_site.

**The biologically meaningful effect is physically implausible.** Across the
entire 3×8×8 grid the *smallest* regime reaching the meaningful bars is:
- >5 pp transient: **M = 3000 occupancy-competent loci** (min), Cas9 = 10^4 molecules.
- >25% t50 delay: **M = 3000 loci** (min), Cas9 = 10^3 molecules.
Nothing below M = 3000 occupancy-competent loci reaches either bar at any volume
or Cas9 count. 3000 distinct loci that each bind and *hold* Cas9, for a single
guide, has no empirical support.

**Inside a generous plausibility envelope** (occupancy-competent loci M ≤ 1000,
active Cas9 ≥ 100 molecules) the maximum effect anywhere (all volumes) is
**4.39 pp transient / 6.3% t50 delay / ~6% depletion**, at the very corner
(M = 1000, Cas9 = 100 molecules ≈ 0.07–0.33 nM, smallest nucleus). At a more
defensible occupancy-competent count (M ≤ 300) every plausible cell is < 0.7 pp
and < 2% t50 delay — negligible. No plausible cell crosses 5 pp or 25% t50, and
final on-target cleavage is unchanged in every plausible cell (it is a transient
delay, not an outcome change).

**Absolute-timescale honesty flag.** The plausible weak effects occur where
on-target t50 is hours (Nc≈100, ~0.33 nM → t50 ≈ 7 h). Pushing to larger effects
requires scarcer Cas9, where t50 stretches to 10^5–10^7 s (days–months) — longer
than RNP lifetime and the cell cycle, making a large % delay biologically moot.

## Answers

1. **Smallest physically scaled regime producing >5 pp transient:** M = 3000
   occupancy-competent loci (6000 target copies) with Cas9 = 10^4 molecules
   (66 nM) at 250 fL (ratio 2M/Nc = 0.6; dOnMax = 5.4 pp). No regime with
   M ≤ 1000 reaches 5 pp at any Cas9 count or volume. The maximum achievable
   inside the plausible envelope is 4.39 pp (M = 1000, Cas9 = 100).
2. **Smallest regime producing >10% t50 delay:** M = 1000 loci with Cas9 = 100
   molecules gives ~6% (below 10%); the ≥10% t50 threshold first appears at
   M = 3000 loci (e.g. Cas9 = 100–1000 molecules, ratio ≥ 6). ≥25% t50 also
   first appears at M = 3000.
3. **Do those regimes require implausible scarcity/burden?** Yes — both. The
   meaningful thresholds require M ≥ 3000 occupancy-competent off-target loci per
   guide (implausible competitor burden). Where a smaller burden is used, they
   additionally require sub-nM active Cas9 (10^1–10^3 molecules), i.e. implausible
   enzyme scarcity, and the delays then unfold over biologically irrelevant
   timescales. The only genuinely plausible corner (M ≈ 1000, Cas9 ≈ 100–1000)
   yields at most a ~2–4 pp transient with unchanged final editing.
4. **Does the project survive molecule-count scaling?** Barely, and only in a
   narrow, marginal corner. The meaningful mechanism (>5 pp / >25% t50) is
   physically implausible; scaling to real copy numbers leaves only a weak
   (1–4 pp), outcome-neutral transient delay confined to a highly promiscuous
   guide (~10^3 occupancy-competent loci) combined with scarce active Cas9. For
   practical genome-engineering relevance this is effectively a kill; continuation
   is justified only if that specific narrow corner is the explicit research
   target, and only after the one controlling unknown — the number of
   occupancy-competent off-target loci per guide relative to active nuclear
   Cas9-RNP molecules — is measured rather than assumed.
